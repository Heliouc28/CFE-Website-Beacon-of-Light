The Sacred Scripture as Stories of Faith

"Faith requires actions"

Inspired Word

Hebrews 11:1
“Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see.”

Characteristics of Christian Faith

Like God's surrender and complete trust to the will of the Father which He ultimately demonstrated in His death on the cross. This features Christian faith and be our example.

God reveals himself in Three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This belief in the triune God is a unique characteristic of the Christian faith.

The love of God motivates us to evangelize the good news. Having such missionary spirit tests if we truly have an authentic faith. It is dynamic, manifested in growing knowledge and love of Christ.

“Faith seek understanding” by St. Anselm. This suggests that by active love of Christ we strive to have a deeper understanding of Christ that can be done through Christian Community.

Faith can be seen with the way you live your life, communicate, and build relationship with others, may it be with brother and sisters in Christ or non-believers. It is important to be able to manifest your faith in everyday life.

Essential Dimension of Faith

An essential part of faith that means having a firm conviction in God and His revelations through Jesus Christ. Accepting and trusting the teachings of Christ that directs how we live our life.

Faith is a commitment to follow God’s will for us. It is putting our faith into action to help establish the Kingdom of God as a kingdom of justice, peace, and love.

Wholeheartedly trusting God and surrendering our lives to His guidance, believing that He loves us, will never deceive us, and will take care of us. Faith is a gift from God's grace, which gives us the strength to believe in Him, trust His plans, and follow His will.
