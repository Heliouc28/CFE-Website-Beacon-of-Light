Reflection Summary

LESSON 1: Introduction to The sacred Scripture

This lesson speaks of the Scripture and how important it is in our daily lives. After all, BIBLE stands for “Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth”. As this serves as our guide every day, we need to study it and know its purpose. Although there have been many versions of the Scripture, they never fail to tell us about the one true God, Jesus Christ. Additionally, in this lesson, we were taught about the different parts and books of the Bible. Overall, we think that knowing about the Scripture will help us see God’s character more clearly, examine our hearts, and grow in wisdom, knowledge and faith.

LESSON 1: Torah

This lesson talks about the Book of Torah and the importance of obedience and law. It is about Moses’ covenant with God and how he was called to carry out the mission of bringing the Israelites out of Egypt. We can also see that God is not just a distant Creator of the universe, but He has helped us every single step of the way. It feels reassuring that God chooses not to leave us in our times of need and even stays, and initiates every single time we need saving. He may not answer us in a manner that involves our way, but He aligns everything to be done in His way as written in His Will.


LESSON 2: Nevi’im

In this module, we learned about the Book of Nevi’im and some of the major prophets who taught us obedience and courage, which was symbolized by the prophets Jonah and Jeremiah. We learned that even though everything seems scary at first, we still need to believe. Sometimes, what’s important is to do it scared instead of not doing it at all. It is about trusting God when we feel reluctant or inadequate. Additionally, Jonah’s story taught us that running away from responsibilities only creates bigger storms and challenges. Meanwhile, Jeremiah shows us that even if we are too young, we will never be “unqualified” to serve God when He calls us.

LESSON 3: Ketuvim

This lesson taught us about the Book of Ketuvim and the wisdom of the Lord. This taught us that true wisdom comes from noticing God in our everyday lives and choosing to follow His ways. It shows us how to live well by working hard, speaking gently to others, and staying away from pride, lies, and bad behavior. Even when we face tough times like Job did, we can get through them by trusting in God's timing and remembering that all good things come from Him. Finally, these Bible stories remind us that God is always walking beside us, guiding us through prayer, worship, and the advice of wise people like Job and the author of Ecclesiastes.

LESSON 1: Mary, the Mother of Jesus and a Woman of Faith

From this lesson, we learned that Mary is our greatest model of faith because she trusted God and said “yes” even when His plans seemed impossible or hard to understand. Her life showed us that real faith isn’t about having all the right answers immediately, but about being open to the Holy Spirit. In addition, we also need to trust God through everyday family struggles and worries. She became not only the Mother of God, but also the loving Mother of the Church and of us all because she said “yes” to Jesus. This inspired us to remain humble and faithful as we follow Christ’s mission.


Lesson 2: John the Baptist, The Forerunner of Jesus

As a group, this passage reminds us that our actions should always show what we truly believe, even when no one is watching or giving us credit for it. After all, God sees your heart and as they say, “Actions speak louder than words.”. Just like John the Baptist, we need to stay humble and focused on pointing people to Jesus because we are called to put aside our pride and help bring others closer to God. In our own lives at school, at home and even in our community, we can follow his example by being honest, standing up for what is fair, and treating everyone with kindness.


Lesson 3: Jesus’ Event in His Public Ministry

This passage and lesson remind us that following Jesus means actively building God’s Kingdom in our world today through love, justice and even welcoming those who are left out. Even if doing what is right leads us to struggle or misunderstanding, Jesus’ Passion and death inspired us to stay honest and faithful to each other and our values. His Resurrection was more than just some historical event. It gives us a brand-new life as God’s children and proves that goodness always triumphs over evil. We realized that we are challenged not just to believe that Jesus is risen, but to live out that hope every day by bringing courage, kindness and light to others in times of darkness.

LESSON 1: The Early Christian Community

This lesson taught us that the Holy Spirit turns our fear into courage like the Apostles at Pentecost. Even if people doubt or make fun of our faith, the Holy Spirit stays with us and helps us keep going. Through Confirmation, we receive special gifts that guide our choices and remind us to focus on God instead of chasing fame. As we strive to finish college and become professionals, we realize that we are called to use these gifts to serve others and show God’s love in our communities and everywhere we go. This is because as Christians, it is our mission and our calling to share our faith and help others to salvation as much as we possibly can.


LESSON 2: The Christian Community Today

As a group, this lesson reminds us that Jesus prayed for His followers to stay united and help the Church grow over time. Through the Philippine Church’s call for renewal, we realized that we need to update our understanding of faith instead of just relying on old habits or traditions. Even though our Filipino devotions to Mary and the saints are beautiful, we must always make sure that Jesus stays at the center of our worship. We are called as Christians to actively participate in our communities and ministries using our talents to help the Church stay united, and focused on Jesus Christ.
